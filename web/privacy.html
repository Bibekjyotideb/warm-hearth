Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

WarmHearth ("the app") is made by Lusane Technologies, a sole proprietorship owned by [Proprietor's full legal name], [Registered business address], Tripura, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data the app and this website handle, why, and the choices you have. It applies to everyone who uses WarmHearth: the senior whose phone runs it, and family members who link their phones.

In short: we collect only what the features need. Photos taken on the senior's phone stay there. Health readings are shared only if the senior agrees, and never used for ads. Linked family see how the phone is (battery, ringer, active recently), never what's on it. Location is shared only with linked family, only during SOS or a detected fall. We never sell data and the app has no ads.

2. Legal basis

We process data with your consent (given in the app, for example when allowing location or linking family), to perform our contract with you (providing the app and the features you bought), to meet legal obligations (tax and payment records), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and working. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we act as the Data Fiduciary.

3. Family linking and consent

Linking lets a family member see and edit the senior's contacts, medicine list and settings, see the phone's status and today's doses, ring the phone or turn its ringer on, send photos and notes, and receive alerts. A link only happens when a code shown on the senior's phone is typed into the family member's phone, and the code is only shown after the senior has read what family will see and do and tapped "I agree". Family members must only link with the senior's knowledge and agreement.

The senior stays in control on their own phone, without needing the settings PIN: home screen → settings button → Privacy & sharing shows what is shared and lets them stop sharing the phone's status, stop sharing watch readings, or unlink every family member at once. WarmHearth is a tool for a person to share safety information with family they choose. It is not designed or marketed to watch someone without their knowledge, and using it that way breaks our terms.

4. Who we share data with

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check), hosted mainly in Google Cloud's Mumbai region (asia-south1).
  • Google Gemini API (via Firebase AI Logic) for the AI features. We use the paid service, under which Google does not use your content to improve its models.
  • Google Play for payments and refunds.
  • Zoho Mail to send and receive support and billing emails.

We do not sell or rent personal data and do not share it for advertising. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect someone's life or safety.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, device, contacts and medicine data: until you delete the account or the app has been inactive for 24 months.
  • SOS, check-in, safety and missed-dose alerts, and dose records: 90 days.
  • Photos and notes from family: 30 days.
  • Health readings: latest values until sharing is stopped; daily summaries 90 days.
  • Phone status: only the latest report is kept (overwritten).
  • Support tickets: 2 years after closing.
  • Purchase records: 8 years, as required for tax records in India (kept without your email after account deletion).
  • Analytics data: 14 months; crash reports: 90 days.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, or name someone to exercise your rights if you cannot. Family members can delete their account in the app (Settings → Delete my account) or at warmhearth.lusanetech.com/delete-account. To erase a senior's phone data, use the same page or uninstall the app and contact us. If you are in the EU, UK or California, you also have the rights given by the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA, including objecting to processing and complaining to your data protection authority.

We answer requests within 30 days. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025, you can also withdraw consent as easily as you gave it (see "Privacy & sharing" above), and complain to our grievance officer (section 10) and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest in Google Cloud. Database access is restricted by security rules so that only the phone's owner and linked family can read it. App Check blocks unofficial copies of the app from our servers. No system is perfectly secure; if a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you without delay (what happened, when, the likely impact, what we've done and what you can do) and report it to the Data Protection Board of India and other authorities as the law requires.

We do not use advertising identifiers, show ads, or use any health data for advertising or product suggestions.

8. Children

WarmHearth is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
