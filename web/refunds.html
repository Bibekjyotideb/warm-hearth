Cancellation & refund policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

WarmHearth Premium is a subscription billed by Google Play, monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Here's how cancelling and refunds work.

Cancel any time

Open Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → WarmHearth → Cancel. You can also get there from WarmHearth: the Premium screen → Manage subscription.

  • During the free trial: cancel before it ends and you pay nothing. We email you 2 days before the trial ends.
  • After that: Premium stays on until the end of the month or year you already paid for, then stops. You aren't charged again.
  • Uninstalling the app or deleting your account does not cancel the subscription. Please cancel in Google Play.

Refunds

Within 48 hours of a charge

Ask Google Play directly: play.google.com → Order history → the WarmHearth charge → Request a refund.

Within 14 days of a charge

Email billing@lusanetech.com with the order ID from your Google Play receipt (starts with GPA.). We refund that charge in full through Google Play, normally within 3 working days. This applies to the first charge after a trial and to yearly renewals.

Later

We look at every request fairly, for example if a Premium feature stopped working and we couldn't fix it. Email billing@lusanetech.com.

What happens after a refund

  • The money returns to the payment method you used, usually in 3–10 working days.
  • Premium switches off and the subscription doesn't renew.
  • The free features keep working, including SOS with family alerts.

Things to know

  • A subscription belongs to the Google account that bought it.
  • Changed phone or reinstalled? On the Premium screen tap Already subscribed? Restore; you don't pay again.
  • Your rights under Indian consumer law and the consumer laws of your country are not affected by this policy.
