Delete your WarmHearth account

You can delete your account and data at any time. This page is the official deletion route for WarmHearth by {{company}}.

Fastest: in the app

  1. Family member: open WarmHearth → the menu on your family screen → Delete my account. This deletes your account, removes you from every linked phone, and deletes your tickets straight away.
  2. Senior's phone: open Settings (PIN) → Delete this phone's data, then uninstall. This deletes the phone's contacts, medicines and alerts from our servers.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account, name and email, linked phones' data you own (contacts, medicine lists, alerts), notification tokens, support tickets.
  • Kept: purchase records (order ID, date, country) for 8 years because Indian tax law requires it, with your email and name removed. Google Play keeps its own payment records.
  • Photos were never uploaded; they are removed when the app is uninstalled.
