Questions

Will it work on my parents' phone?

Any Android phone running Android 8 or newer. Their apps, WhatsApp and photos stay exactly where they are; WarmHearth only changes the home screen.

Can they go back to the old home screen?

Yes. Settings → "Switch to the old home screen" (behind the PIN) brings back the phone's normal launcher. Nothing is deleted.

Is WarmHearth an emergency service?

No. SOS alerts family and calls their emergency contact, but it depends on the phone having battery and network. It does not replace 112 / 911, and fall detection is a helper, not a medical device.

Who can see their location?

Only family members they linked, and only when SOS is pressed or a fall is detected. See the privacy policy.

Do the AI features read their messages?

Only the single message or photo they choose to check, at the moment they tap Check or Read. It's sent to Google's Gemini service for the answer and isn't stored by us.
