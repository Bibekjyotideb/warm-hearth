Questions

Will it work on my parents' phone?

Any Android phone running Android 8 or newer. The scam call shield needs Android 10 or newer. Their apps, WhatsApp and photos stay exactly where they are; WarmHearth only changes the home screen.

Do I need an iPhone or Android for the family app?

The family app is on Android today. An iPhone version is planned.

How does the daily check-in work?

Each morning a card on their home screen says "Let your family know you're OK". One tap and you get a gentle notification. If they haven't tapped it by the time you chose, you get an alert and can ring their phone from it.

Can they go back to the old home screen?

Yes. Settings → "Switch to the old home screen" (behind the PIN) brings back the phone's normal launcher. Nothing is deleted.

Is WarmHearth an emergency service?

No. SOS alerts family and calls their emergency contact, but it depends on the phone having battery and network. It does not replace 112 / 911, and fall detection is a helper, not a medical device.

Who can see what?

Only family members they linked. They see the phone's status (battery, ringer, last used), check-ins, medicine doses and alerts. Location only when SOS is pressed or a fall is detected. See the privacy policy.

Do the AI features read their messages?

Only the single message or photo they choose to check, at the moment they tap Check or Read. It's sent to Google's Gemini service for the answer and isn't stored by us.
