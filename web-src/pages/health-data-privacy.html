Consumer health data privacy policy

Last updated: {{updated}}

This policy is for people in Washington State (My Health My Data Act), Nevada (SB 370), Connecticut and other US states with consumer health data laws. It adds to our main privacy policy and explains which health-related data WarmHearth handles, why, and how you control it. WarmHearth is not an emergency service or a medical device.

1. The consumer health data we collect

  • Medicines: medicine names, doses and reminder times that you or linked family enter, and whether each dose was marked taken or missed.
  • Watch and fitness band readings (only if you turn on sharing and allow Health Connect): heart rate, resting heart rate, steps, sleep, blood oxygen, blood pressure, blood glucose and weight, read-only.
  • Safety events: SOS presses, a possible fall detected by the phone, and daily "I'm OK" check-ins, which can show something about your health.
  • Precise location only when an SOS or fall alert is sent, so family can find you.

2. Where it comes from

From you on your phone, from family members you have linked (they can add medicines and contacts for you), from your phone's sensors, and from Health Connect on your phone (which gets data from your watch or fitness app).

3. Why we collect and use it

Only to provide the features you asked for: medicine reminders, showing today's doses and readings to the family members you linked, and sending safety alerts. We never use consumer health data for advertising, for product suggestions, for credit, insurance or employment decisions, and we never sell it.

4. Your consent

We collect health readings only after you agree in the app (a separate consent screen before Health Connect). We share consumer health data with your family members only after a separate consent: the "What your family will see" screen you agree to before linking. You can withdraw either consent at any time, without a PIN, in Settings → Privacy & sharing, which stops the collection or sharing straight away.

5. Who we share it with

  • The family members you linked, as described above.
  • Our processors, who handle data only on our instructions: Google Firebase / Google Cloud (storage and servers) and the Google Gemini API (only for the AI medicine-label reader, when you use it).
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We do not sell consumer health data and do not share it with any other third party.

6. Your rights

You can confirm whether we hold your consumer health data, see it and the list of everyone we shared it with, withdraw consent, and have it deleted (including from our processors). Use warmhearth.lusanetech.com/delete-account or email {{privacy_contact}}. We answer within 45 days. If we refuse, you can appeal by replying to our answer; if you are still unhappy, you can contact your state attorney general (in Washington: atg.wa.gov).

7. How long we keep it

Dose records, safety events and daily reading summaries: 90 days. Latest readings: until you stop sharing. Medicine list: until you delete it or your account.
