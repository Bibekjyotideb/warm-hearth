Quick fixes

The medicine alarm didn't ring

On the senior's phone open Settings (PIN) and tap Allow on-time medicine alarms and Allow full-screen alerts if you see them. On Xiaomi, Redmi, Realme, Oppo and Vivo phones also turn off battery saving for WarmHearth: long-press the WarmHearth icon → App info → Battery → No restrictions.

I didn't get an SOS alert

Check that your phone is linked (it should be listed in the app), that notifications are allowed for WarmHearth on your phone, and that the senior's phone has internet. SOS also calls their first emergency contact directly.

The home screen went back to the old one

Press the Home button. If Android asks which app to use, choose WarmHearth and "Always". Or open WarmHearth Settings → Make it the home screen.

I forgot the settings PIN

Send us a ticket from the family phone that's linked to it; we'll walk you through resetting it.

I bought the unlock but it isn't showing

Open the unlock screen and tap Already bought? Restore, signed in with the Google account you paid with. Still stuck? Choose "Billing" below and include your order ID (starts with GPA.).
