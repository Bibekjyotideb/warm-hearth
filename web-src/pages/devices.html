Watches and bands that work with WarmHearth

With one of these on your parent's wrist, WarmHearth's family app shows their heart rate, steps, sleep and more, next to their phone's status and today's medicines.

How it connects: set the watch up on your parent's phone with its own app, turn on Health Connect in that app, then on their phone open WarmHearth Settings → Watch & health readings → Share. They can stop sharing at any time.

Before you buy another brand

Many budget watches only keep readings in their own app. Check that the brand's app lists Health Connect in its settings or Play Store page; if it does, WarmHearth can show its readings.

Fall detection on a watch calls the watch's own emergency contacts. WarmHearth's SOS and fall detection on the phone still alert you as usual.
